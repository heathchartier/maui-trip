Good to Know

No Gas in Hana

Fill all 3 vehicles in Kahului before getting on Hwy 36. The next gas after Pa'ia is back in Kahului, 2+ hours away.

No Cell Signal on Hana Road

Signal drops to near zero after Garden of Eden. Screenshot this guide and download offline Google Maps for Pa'ia → Hana before leaving.

Lahaina Fire Recovery

The 2023 wildfire destroyed much of historic Front Street. Many shops are reopening — call ahead before visiting specific businesses.

Reef-Safe Sunscreen

Required at all Hawaii state parks. Bring or buy oxybenzone-free sunscreen before arriving.

Turtles — 10-Foot Rule

Hawaiian green sea turtles are federally protected. You must stay 10 feet away. Don't touch, chase, or block their path. Fines apply.
