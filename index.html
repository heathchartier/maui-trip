Black Rock Snorkel + Turtles — Pu'u Keka'a

Hawaiian green sea turtles regularly rest and feed in the shallows right off the Sheraton beach at Black Rock — one of the most reliable turtle spots in all of West Maui. Good to Know

No Gas in Hana

Fill all 3 vehicles in Kahului before Hwy 36. Next gas after Pa'ia is 2+ hours away.

No Cell Signal on Hana Road

Signal drops after Garden of Eden. Download Google Maps offline for Pa'ia→Hana before leaving the hotel.

Lahaina Fire Recovery

August 2023 wildfire destroyed much of Front Street. Many shops reopening — call ahead before visiting.

Reef-Safe Sunscreen

Required at all Hawaii state parks. Bring or buy oxybenzone-free sunscreen.

10-Foot Rule for Turtles

Hawaiian green sea turtles are federally protected. Stay 10 feet away — fines apply.
